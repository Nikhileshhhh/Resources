# Channel strategy (working draft, 2026-10-18)

## Niche and promise
**Cinematic animated true-history thrillers**: extraordinary survival, ingenious escapes, and decisions that changed what happened next.
Promise: *"Extraordinary real events, told with suspense and explained clearly in about nine minutes."*

This is a **starting hypothesis**, not a proven winner. No niche guarantees reach; what we can control is viewer satisfaction (a clear promise, delivered), packaging, retention and a sustainable cadence.

## Why this niche fits your resources
- **Production:** the pipeline's illustrated 2D style (vector characters, maps, documents) suits stories that turn on *places, routes, decisions and documents*. Escapes and survival journeys map naturally onto animated maps and a small recurring cast. No stock footage or paid image generation is needed.
- **Cost:** a nine-minute episode costs about ₹25–40 of narration and QA transcription on AI Credits (episode 1 measured about ₹37 including tests). Rendering is local CPU time (about 1.5–2 h).
- **Storytelling:** escape stories have a built-in question ("how did they get out?") and a payoff. That supports retention without manufactured cliffhangers.
- **Evidence culture:** we narrate disputed points openly (legend vs letters). That is a distinctive angle against retellings that repeat legends as fact.

## Audience
Accessible English for teens and adults, India first, with worldwide English speakers in mind. Indian and world history, about 50/50 over time. A general-audience channel: **not** made for kids. Language is configurable per episode (`LANGUAGE`); one language per channel, never mixed within a video.

## Content mix
About 80% evergreen stories and 20% timely angles (documented anniversaries, real discoveries), always inside the niche. Multipart only when the material genuinely fills more than one complete episode.

For curious teens and adults who love a gripping true story, **The Narrow Hour** tells history's narrowest escapes and boldest decisions as illustrated animated thrillers. Each runs about nine minutes and is checked against the sources, including where the legend and the letters disagree.
