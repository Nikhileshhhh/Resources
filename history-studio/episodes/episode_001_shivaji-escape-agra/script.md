> What happened next... grew into legend. For generations, Shivaji's flight was told as a long pilgrimage, through Benares, Gaya, even Puri. The chronicles say he smeared himself with ash, disguised as a wandering ascetic. [serious] But in nineteen thirty-nine, letters were found in the Jaipur archives. Spy reports quoted in them said Shivaji reached his fort just twenty-five days after slipping out of Agra. Twenty-five days, over more than a thousand kilometres, meant hard, fast travel on hidden paths. [softly] And it cost him. He lay ill for weeks after he got home.
