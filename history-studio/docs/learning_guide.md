# Learning after publication

Fill `analytics_template.csv` from **YouTube Studio → Analytics** (use *Advanced mode* exports) at **24 h, 72 h, 7 days and 28 days**. Use your actual exports only. Never estimate or invent numbers.

## Read the numbers cautiously
- Early samples are small. A few hundred impressions can swing CTR by several points by chance.
- Compare videos **of similar age** and **similar traffic sources** (Browse vs Search vs Suggested behave differently).
- There is **no universal "good" CTR or retention**. YouTube itself says they vary by format and traffic source. Compare against your own channel's typical range once you have 5–10 videos.

## Diagnose with pairs of metrics
| Pattern | Likely cause | What to try next |
|---|---|---|
| Low impressions CTR, normal retention once people click | **Packaging** (title or thumbnail) | Test another thumbnail with *Test & compare* if your channel has it; otherwise change one element and note it as an uncontrolled change |
| Normal CTR, sharp drop in the first 30 s | **Opening** doesn't match the promise, or starts too slowly | Get to the specific situation faster; make sure the first line pays off the title |
| High CTR but heavy early drop and comments saying "not what I expected" | **Misleading title** | Make the title truthful to what the video delivers (it should be anyway) |
| Steady decline through the middle | **Confusing or slow script** (too many names, not enough cause and effect) | Fewer names, a clearer stakes reminder, a map at each geographic jump |
| Good retention and CTR but very few impressions | **Distribution** hasn't started (new channel) or the **topic** draws little interest on Browse | Wait for more data before judging; compare Search vs Browse impressions |
| One episode strong, the next ones weak | **Single strong topic**, not yet a repeatable direction | Look for what the strong one did differently (topic region, title framing, opening) and repeat that one variable |

## After each review
Recommend **one or two concrete changes** for the next episode, for example "open on the guard ring, not the fort" or "name at most five people". Note the change in the CSV's `change_tested` column. Avoid changing many things at once: you won't know what worked. Manual title or thumbnail swaps without a controlled test are useful hints, not proof.

## Scheduling
After about 30 days, check *Audience → When your viewers are on YouTube* and the geography report. Move the 18:30 IST slot only if your own viewers are clearly active at other times. No posting time guarantees long-term performance.

## Monetization reality check
Joining YPP requires 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months (or the Shorts threshold) and a policy review. Long-form length alone doesn't make a channel eligible to earn money.
