Agra, sixteen sixty-six. For three months, Shivaji and his nine-year-old son, Sambhaji, have been held in a house watched by the emperor Aurangzeb's guards. Their safety rests on the word of Ram Singh, son of Mirza Raja Jai Singh. Home, the hill fort of Rajgad, lies more than a thousand kilometres to the south. And every evening, large baskets of sweets are carried out of the house.
