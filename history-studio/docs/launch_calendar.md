# 12-episode launch calendar (proposal, Asia/Kolkata, UTC+05:30)

**Test schedule:** Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at **18:30 IST** (13:00 UTC), one video per scheduled day and none on other days. Upload each video **privately at least 24 h earlier** so HD processing and checks finish. Uploading is not publishing: set **Schedule → Public** for the slot.

This is a starting experiment, not a proven optimal time. Once there is meaningful data, refine it using *Analytics → Audience → When your viewers are on YouTube* and viewer geography.

**Do not publish a weak episode to fill a slot.** Slip the calendar instead. Dates below assume the research and render pace of episode 1 (about one working day per episode); adjust to what's sustainable.
